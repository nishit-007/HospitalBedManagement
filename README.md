# 🏥 Hospital Bed Management & Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project built using **MySQL** and **Microsoft Power BI** to analyse hospital operations, monitor bed utilisation, optimise capacity planning, and evaluate doctor and department performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Hospitals need timely operational insights to efficiently manage admissions, bed occupancy, department utilisation, and doctor workload.

This project demonstrates a complete BI workflow starting from database design in **MySQL** to interactive dashboard development in **Power BI**, enabling hospital administrators to monitor key performance indicators and support operational decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor hospital admissions and patient flow.
- Analyse bed occupancy and utilisation trends.
- Evaluate department-wise performance.
- Identify top-performing doctors based on admissions.
- Support hospital capacity planning through occupancy analysis.

# 📌 Key Business Insights

- Cardiology recorded the highest number of admissions.
- Average hospital bed utilisation remained around **65%**, indicating available capacity while maintaining healthy occupancy.
- Morning shifts handled the highest share of admissions.
- General Medicine maintained the largest bed capacity.
- Occupancy peaked during the middle months of the year, highlighting periods of increased hospital demand.
